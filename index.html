How it all began

Love at first sight
is real.

Their story began at their first job, where Cedrix first caught a glimpse of Monessa and was instantly drawn to her.

From that moment on, he found little opportunities to say hello, start a conversation, and simply be around her. He greeted her almost every day, smiled whenever he saw her, and did his best to get her attention. But winning Monessa’s heart was not something that happened overnight.

Little by little, those familiar hellos became longer conversations. The shared moments became something they both looked forward to. And somewhere along the way, Monessa began to see Cedrix not just as the familiar face who always found a reason to say hello, but as someone who had quietly and consistently made an effort to be part of her life.

What started as Cedrix’s determined pursuit slowly blossomed into a friendship, then a love story—one built over time, through countless small moments, laughter, shared adventures, and the choice to keep choosing each other.

And after years of growing together, she finally said yes—to him, and to a lifetime together.

Now, they are ready to begin their next chapter as husband and wife.
