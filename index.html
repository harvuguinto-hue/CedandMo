Our Story
Our Love Story

Love at first sight
is real.

Our story began at our first job. Ced still remembers the first time he caught a glimpse of Mo and was instantly drawn to her. From there, he found every little opportunity to say hello, start a conversation, or simply be around her.

A hello here, a smile there, and somehow, Ced always found a reason to cross Mo’s path. Of course, winning her heart wasn’t something that happened overnight. Little by little, those familiar hellos turned into longer conversations. started looking forward to seeing each other and finding more reasons to spend time together.

What began as a friendship slowly became something more, and before we knew it, we were falling in love. Over the years, we built a life filled with countless little moments, lots of laughter, shared adventures, and memories we’ll always carry with us. Through every chapter, we grew together and kept choosing each other. Somewhere along the way, there was no longer just “Ced” and “Mo.” There was “us.” Years later, Ced asked the biggest question of all.

And Mo said yes. Yes to everything that brought us here, and yes to everything still to come. Now, we get to begin our next chapter together as husband and wife.
